Coach 5.0 Personal: Privacy

This is a single-user personal tool. It reads one person's resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep data from the Google Health API, using read-only access, and writes it to that person's own Google Sheet.

The data is not shared with, sold to, or used by anyone else. Nothing is collected from any other user. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
